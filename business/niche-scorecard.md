# Niche scorecard (Phase 1, step 1)

Scored 1–5 per criterion. Max 20.

| # | Niche | Pain fixable in <2 wks | Ability to pay | Cheap to reach | Your unfair advantage | Total |
|---|-------|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Small/mid nonprofits on Salesforce NPSP, Raiser's Edge NXT, or a messy CRM, no ops staff | 5 | 3 | 4 | 5 | **17** |
| 2 | Membership associations & chambers (Wild Apricot, GrowthZone, MemberClicks) | 4 | 3 | 4 | 4 | **15** |
| 3 | Small B2B teams on HubSpot (10–50 staff, no RevOps hire) | 4 | 5 | 2 | 4 | **15** |
| 4 | Home services / trades (roofing, auto body, HVAC) | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | **14** |
| 5 | Professional services (accountants, agencies, real estate, insurance) | 3 | 4 | 3 | 2 | **12** |

## Why each scored that way

1. **Nonprofits.** Gift imports (Stripe/PayPal/forms into CRM), dedupe, acknowledgments, board reports: fixable in days. Budgets are tight, but $1.5–3k is in range for $1–10M orgs. Targeting is free: IRS 990 data via ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer and Candid gives budget size, and staff are public. You've done this exact work at scale. Risks: employer overlap (see guardrails), and year-end giving season (mid-Nov to Dec) cuts reply rates.
2. **Associations/chambers.** Same nonprofit muscle (renewals, event regs into CRM, reporting). Chambers can also refer local businesses to you. It's a small pool, though, and largely a nonprofit variant, so testing it alone wouldn't teach you much new.
3. **B2B HubSpot.** They have the most money and the most pain (lead routing, enrichment, dedupe, pipeline reporting). They're hard to find by pain signal, and HubSpot partner agencies crowd the space. Your integrations background shows up best here.
4. **Trades.** Fast pain (speed-to-lead, quote follow-up, review requests), good cash, and you have 2 site references. But every GoHighLevel agency spams them, and they answer phones more than email.
5. **Professional services.** Too broad. Real estate and insurance are buried in vendors, and accounting firms are in tax season Jan–Apr. **Use bookkeepers and accountants as referral partners, not targets.**

## Recommendation: test 1, 3, 4
Each one tests a different hypothesis:
- **Nonprofits** = advantage-led (primary bet, ~50% of effort)
- **B2B HubSpot** = budget-led (~25%)
- **Trades** = reference/network-led (~25%)

Cut: professional services (they become partners). Park: associations (they can reuse the nonprofit page later).

## Guardrails (non-negotiable)
- Read your employment agreement for moonlighting, non-solicit, and IP clauses before anything goes public.
- Suppress from every list any org you know or suspect is an employer customer. Never use employer data.
- No SMS fundraising, auctions, or workplace giving, even as add-ons.
