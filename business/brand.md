## Recommendation: **Stop Retyping** (stopretyping.com)
- It names the pain in two words. A roofer, an agency owner, and a sales ops lead all get it instantly.
- It doesn't mention AI (that's a feature, not a bug) and doesn't box you into one niche.
- It's the exact .com with no "hq/co/get" hacks, and it reads well in a subject line ("Stop retyping leads into Jobber").
- Risk: it sounds data-entry-only. That's fine, because "follow-ups that send themselves" is the same promise one step further.
- Runner-up: Busywork Co (warmer, vaguer).

**Before buying:** run a 2-minute search at USPTO (tmsearch.uspto.gov) for "stop retyping" and check that the LinkedIn page name is free.

## Positioning (one line)
> Stop Retyping connects the tools your team already pays for, so data gets entered once and follow-ups never slip. Fixed price, live in two weeks.

## Voice & tone
- Plain words a business owner would say out loud. "Your leads sit for 6 hours," not "optimize lead velocity."
- Specific beats clever. Name the tool, the step, and the minutes saved.
- AI is mentioned only as a means ("a draft summary you edit"), never as the headline. No "revolutionize," "unlock," "leverage," "seamless," or "AI-powered."
- First person ("I"), not a fake "we." You're one person, and that's the selling point.
- Short sentences, with no exclamation points.
- Always show the before → after.

## Visual direction
- **Colors:** paper `#FAF8F5`, ink `#1C1F23`, muted `#5F6670`, one accent in safety orange `#E8590C` (tool-shop, not tech-startup); dark mode uses ink `#F2EFEA` on `#15171A`
- **Type:** Inter Tight (headings, bold), Inter (body), JetBrains Mono (before/after snippets). All are free on Google Fonts.
- **Feel:** a clean work order, not a SaaS landing page. No gradients, no robot imagery; real screenshots of workflows instead.
