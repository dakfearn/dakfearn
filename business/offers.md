# Offers (Phase 1, step 2)

Rules for every offer: fixed scope, fixed price, 50% upfront and 50% at go-live, and built on tools the client already pays for (Zapier/Make/n8n plus their CRM). The client owns all accounts.
**Founding price:** the first 2 clients per niche get 30% off in exchange for a case study and testimonial. This gives you proof fast.

## 1. B2B HubSpot teams: **HubSpot Cleanup + Lead Routing Sprint** · $1,800 · live in 10 business days
- Dedupe and merge contacts/companies, plus property cleanup (top 20 fields)
- Form → enrich → route to the right owner → task + follow-up email within 5 min
- AI lead summary on each new inbound ("who they are, what they asked, suggested reply"), written to the contact record
- One pipeline dashboard leadership actually opens
- Founding price: $1,260
- **Lead magnet:** *Free 20-min HubSpot Busywork Teardown.* They screen-share and get 3 automations ranked by hours saved, plus a 1-page written follow-up.

## 2. Trades (roofing, auto body, HVAC): **Speed-to-Lead System** · $1,200 setup · live in 7 days
- Every web form, missed call, and Google Business message → instant text + email reply ("Got it, here's what happens next")
- Lead lands in their CRM (Jobber / Housecall Pro / or a Google Sheet if they have nothing) and the owner gets pinged
- Quote follow-ups on day 2 / 5 / 10 until they win or lose
- Review request auto-sent when the job is marked done
- Optional: $99/mo monitoring and tweaks (recurring revenue, can be cancelled any time)
- Founding price: $840
- **Lead magnet:** *Free Lead Leak Check.* I submit a test lead on your website, time the response, and send you a 2-minute video of what happened. It runs async, needs no call, and doubles as the cold-email hook.

## 3. Agencies (5–30 staff): **Client Ops Autopilot** · $1,500 · live in 10 business days
- Signed proposal → project + task template in their PM tool (ClickUp / Asana / Monday), shared client folder, and kickoff email
- Invoice drafted in QuickBooks/Xero on the schedule in the proposal
- Monthly client report: data pulled automatically, with an AI-drafted summary for the account manager to edit (they never send it raw)
- Founding price: $1,050
- **Lead magnet:** *Free 20-min Agency Ops Teardown.* Same format as the HubSpot one.

## Out of scope (say no politely)
Custom apps, website rebuilds (unless trades and paid separately), ongoing "AI strategy" retainers, anything SMS-fundraising/auction/workplace-giving.
